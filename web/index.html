MESHPUTER

A Meshtastic messenger for the M5Stack Cardputer and Tab5.

Pair it over Bluetooth with the Meshtastic radio you already own and chat over LoRa from a real keyboard or the Tab5's touch screen. No phone, no Wi-Fi, no extra radio hardware.

Your browser can't flash devices. Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Open this page over HTTPS to install.

Plug the Cardputer or Tab5 in with a USB-C data cable, press Install, pick the serial port; the right firmware is chosen for the device. Updating keeps your paired radio and settings.

What you need

  1. An M5Stack Cardputer (v1.1), Cardputer ADV or Tab5.
  2. Any Meshtastic radio with Bluetooth — Heltec, T-Beam, RAK, T-Echo, Station G2… running stock firmware.
  3. Its Bluetooth PIN (default 123456; radios with a screen show it).

What it does

Find & pairScans for radios nearby, pairs with the PIN, reconnects on its own.
ChatDirect messages and channels, with sent / relayed / delivered ticks.
IdentitySee who anyone is — name, hardware, hops, signal, encryption. Edit your own name.
HistoryConversations saved to the SD card, per radio.
Tab5 touch UIIts own layout for the 5" screen: side by side in landscape, one page at a time in portrait, turning with the device.
EmojiPicker sends real emoji, so phones see them too. Quick replies built in.
Battery first80 MHz CPU, Wi-Fi off unless you turn Internet on, Bluetooth only when needed, screen sleeps.

Other ways to install

Download the single image ({{VERSION}}) for the Cardputer or the Tab5 for M5Burner, M5Launcher (as full firmware) or esptool:

esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x0 meshputer-cardputer.bin
esptool.py --chip esp32p4 write_flash 0x0 meshputer-tab5.bin

The single image resets saved settings; the browser installer above keeps them.
